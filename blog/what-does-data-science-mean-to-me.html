What does data science mean to me?

August 19, 2026

What is data science to me?

Data science is a broad and somewhat nebulous term to me. Every other form of science is almost self explanatory from the name alone: earth science, health science, etc, because most people already have an understanding of the topic. Study of the earth, study of health, study of data. But what is data?

When you say that data is anything and everything in recorded history, that there's an unimaginable amount of data already stored and that that amount is rising exponentially every year, it's such a grand scale of things that it feels hard to condense into a simple, concise definition.

Towards that effort, I will say that it is the process of dissecting information, modifying it, analyzing it to derive key insights, and converting those insights into a form that anybody could understand in plain speech.

I find it useful to say a specific field when talking about data science. I am interested in healthcare for example, but there's also political, business, many different industries with enough of their own quirks that you could be analyzing completely different things given each context. It's exciting.

Why am I in data science?

As a child I was very math-inclined, and I took an interest in numbers. For some reason they fascinated me and I would spend hours solving grade-level math problems until I got bored and started working ahead.

That interest died out somewhat as I grew and became much more social, started playing football, and didn't take my studies seriously enough, spending basically all of my time at home playing video games and reading manga. I dropped out of high school at 16 during COVID and explored some options for about 2 years before returning to school to pursue an Associate's.

As I was looking through the Wake Tech Community College catalog, I happened to see a program in Business Analytics and I chose it on a whim, but thinking about it in retrospect I might've remembered some of that fascination in numbers and wanted to explore it again. I got accepted for that fall, graduated 2 years later and enjoyed the topic enough that I transferred to UNCC's Data Science program, moving 3 hours from my hometown of Raleigh.
